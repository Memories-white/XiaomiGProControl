# XiaomiGProControl v0.13.18

## 中文

- 新增 ROG 和拯救者风格外观。两套主题有各自的设备概览，启动或切换主题时会按显示缩放和屏幕可用区域调整窗口尺寸。
- 修复拯救者概览在默认尺寸下仍出现滚动条的问题。窗口缩小时仍可滚动查看全部卡片。
- 侧边栏支持展开和收起，恢复选中项的圆角、高亮条和图标回弹动画。二级页面可从内容区左上角返回。
- 重排硬件详情页，将虚拟显示适配器收进折叠区。设置新增隐藏常规提示的选项。
- 切换显卡输出模式后自动重新读取固件，并在卡片内提示确认结果和重启要求。

下载 `GProControl-0.13.18-Setup-x64.exe` 安装。覆盖安装沿用原目录，可用 `SHA256SUMS.txt` 校验安装包。

## English

- Added ROG- and Legion-inspired appearances, each with its own dashboard. The window adapts to display scaling and available screen space on launch and when switching appearances.
- Fixed the scrollbar on the Legion dashboard at its default window size. The dashboard remains scrollable when resized to a smaller window.
- The sidebar can collapse and expand, with rounded selection highlights, an accent strip, and icon bounce animations. Subpages place the Back button at the top left of the content area.
- Redesigned hardware details and grouped virtual display adapters in a collapsible section. Routine notices can now be hidden in Settings.
- After a GPU output mode change, the app rereads the firmware and shows the confirmation status and restart requirement in the card.

Download `GProControl-0.13.18-Setup-x64.exe`. Upgrading reuses the existing installation directory. Verify the installer with `SHA256SUMS.txt`.
